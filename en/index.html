Current research for the German healthcare system

One search term, every database. New studies are added each morning, summarised in German.

Or go straight to: Topics in care ↓ Disease areas ↓ New studies ↓

  • 15 topic hubs
  • updated daily
  • free, no registration

A note on language The hubs are written in German: the interface, the search glossary and the daily study summaries. The literature behind them is international and English-language — what the hubs add is the bridge into German. If you read German, everything here is open to you at no cost and without registration. If you do not, our English-language journal Monitor Healthcare Research is the better starting point.

How it works

The same in every hub, free and without registration.

  1. Enter a term

    In German or English. The glossary translates German terms for the international databases.

  2. Search every database

    Each tile opens the live search in its source. Where a source does not allow that, the tile leads to its start page and says so.

  3. New studies every morning

    Selected by the editors with AI support and summarised in German. By e-mail every day on request.

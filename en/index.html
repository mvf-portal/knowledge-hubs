The Knowledge Hubs

Research portals for the German healthcare system: one search term opens the live search in every relevant database — and each morning brings a new selection of studies. This page explains what the hubs do; the hubs themselves are in German.

DEEN

A note on language The hubs are written in German: the interface, the search glossary and the daily study summaries. The literature behind them is international and English-language — what the hubs add is the bridge into German. If you read German, everything here is open to you at no cost and without registration. If you do not, our English-language journal Monitor Healthcare Research is the better starting point.

15 hubs are online

Together 1189 databases, 563 of them with a genuine live search; 2325 search terms in the German–English glossary. Each hub stands on its own and has its own daily newsletter. The search spaces do touch — depending on the hub, 3 to 28 per cent of the works found also fall within the field of another (checked 08/2026); the upper figure concerns health literacy and AI alone, and without the AI hub the range reaches only 13 per cent. In the daily selection this barely shows: of 1949 studies delivered so far, 70 have appeared in more than one hub.

What all hubs have in common

  • One search, every databaseOne term is enough: each tile opens the matching live search in a new tab. Where a source does not allow that, the tile leads to the start page — and says so.
  • Search in German, find in EnglishA curated search glossary translates the German technical term for the international databases; German-language sources keep it.
  • New studies every morningAn AI selects from the new entries in PubMed what matters for the German healthcare system and summarises it in German — with the result figures and an assessment of how far it transfers.
  • Newsletter and downloadsThe daily selection comes by e-mail on request, plus as a Word and an Excel file. Every newsletter page also lets you subscribe to the newsletters of the other hubs.
  • No cookies, no third-party servicesStatic pages without third-party analytics. Fonts and images sit on our own server. How often the pages are opened we count ourselves — without cookies and without storing IP addresses.
  • Free and openNo account, no registration, no paywall. Where a database itself requires a licence, the tile is marked as licence-restricted.
